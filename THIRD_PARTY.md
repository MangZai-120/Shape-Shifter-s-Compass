# Knowledge sources

Compass code remains under its existing MIT license. Knowledge and upstream text retain their respective original licenses.

- SSC release resources: Shape Shifter Curse by Onixary and contributors. Separate SSC 1.9.2 and 1.10.0 snapshots; exact JAR hashes are in `knowledge/generated/manifest.json`. Names, mechanics and default data are extracted from the corresponding local release. Narrative/appearance text and assets are not imported.
- SSCA source/resources: MangZai-120 and contributors, branches recorded by commit and version in the manifest. Public gameplay data and non-story descriptions are indexed; no story books, loot data, world rune seeds or solved spell/rune layouts are imported. The original project distinguishes licenses for code and narrative text; this index does not relicense those inputs.
- SSC detailed Wiki: [LynxCatTheThird/SSC-wiki](https://github.com/LynxCatTheThird/SSC-wiki), contributors, commit `f279b358aaf759bab314868f18ecafaf9ed22182`. Fourteen Chinese SSC pages from `docs/ssc/` are adapted under **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. Adaptations remove frontmatter and interactive charts, flatten headings with parent context, and correct the Minecraft time label at 23000 ticks to 05:00 the next day. Each section includes a permanent source URL. These adapted pages retain CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; the full license is bundled at `assets/ssc_compass/knowledge/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt`.
- MC百科: [SSC](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/20760.html) and [SSCA](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/24327.html), consulted 2026-10-15 for brief independent source notes and links. The old unversioned mirrors are excluded from the built mod. No new full-page mirrors are bundled.

The detailed Wiki snapshot documents SSC 1.10.0. It is excluded from answers in SSC 1.9.2 environments. Server data packs/configuration may override upstream defaults.
